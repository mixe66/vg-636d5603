What we know about this car

Historics sold this car in March 2019 for £25.760, one of the highest results in the series. Red with beige leather, genuine right-hand-drive version, 27,592 miles, engine number 110F0002095824. The car was restored by a renowned London restorer.

Historics described the car as once owned by nightclub owner Peter Stringfellow. Note: other auction texts link Stringfellow to the model in general, not firmly to this car. Treat it as claimed, not proven.

Date question resolved: Historics states ‘originally registered 1 October 1973’. That confirms the date in the Drive folder name against the VIN tab, which kept 1972. Built around 1972, first registration in the UK October 1973.

Is the car still on the road?

No, not currently. DVLA check (29 August 2026): SORN, declared off the road. Last registration document 11 October 2019.

Distinguishing features

From the photo analysis: two wing mirrors instead of one, and a beige interior instead of the standard black version. The register also mentions whitewall tyres and a chrome grille insert.
