What we know about this car

According to the seller this Gamine was built in 1969 and used as a summer car on the Amalfi Coast. The later owner bought it in southern Italy in 1979. A photo from that year, captioned ‘1979 Salerno Italy’ by hand, shows it with a hoop behind the seats and a ‘Jeans’ sticker on the door. The register lists the Italian registration SA187903.

In September 1980 the buyer drove it back to England himself, as a personal import. The odometer then read 57,000 km. In August 1984 it received its first British registration, with the plate MFX 25G. In 1986 came a bare-metal respray in De Tomaso red, new seats in white leather with red piping, new carpets over the original rubber mats and a roof with side screens.

After that, according to the seller, it was always garaged, with only 950 miles (about 1,530 km) added. That fits the latest reading of 58,542 km: 57,000 km plus 1,530 km is 58,530 km.

What does not add up

First registration. Car & Classic gives 21 June 1969. The 2023 eBay listing gives 21 June 1971, and that is also the date in the register. DVLA gives a year of manufacture of 1969. Day and month match, only the year differs. Which one is right cannot be established without the Italian papers.

Overhaul. The Augsburg listing reports a complete overhaul in 2020: engine, mechanicals, paint, electrics and upholstery. That does not fit what the seller wrote from 2023 through 2025: an engine with its original patina, the last paintwork in 1986 and only 950 miles since 1980. Who had this work done, and when, is not known.

Number of owners. The listings give 2 previous owners (2023), 4 previous owners (eBay, 2024) and 3 keepers (Augsburg). The seller himself always wrote that he had owned the car since 1979. The number depends on what an advertiser fills in.

Registration on the 1986 photo. The plate on the Bournemouth photo is only partly legible and begins with ‘?FX 6’. That does not match MFX 25G. The photo is too blurry to conclude anything from it.

Is the car still on the road?

Probably, but that cannot be confirmed. DVLA check (29 August 2026): untaxed since 6 June 2025, no MOT record (exempt, over 40 years old), export marker YES, latest registration certificate 3 November 2023. The AutoScout24 listing places the car in Augsburg, which fits that export marker. A German registration is not known.

Distinguishing features

From the register's photo analysis: the seat upholstery is non-standard, with a diamond pattern instead of the standard vertical stitching.

The 2025 auction text also mentions a chrome rear roll hoop, a three-spoke leather-rimmed steering wheel with a Fiat centre boss, steel wheels with chrome hub caps and a matching spare, electric screen washers and four Pioneer speakers. The roof is off-white, with matching side screens. There is localised bubbling on the boot lid, front wing and scuttle, and a dent in the engine cover.

The 1979 photo shows a hoop behind the seats and a ‘Jeans’ sticker on the door. Neither can be seen in the later photos.
