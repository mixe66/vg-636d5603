What we know about this car

This car was offered by Luzzago 1975, a classic-car dealer in Brescia. White with red interior, 15,145 km, matching numbers and the original booklet from first registration. Equipment includes a sunroof, black wheels and drum brakes. In the register the car is listed under the name of Andreas Weber, with a map pin near Karlsfeld close to Munich; previous registration MI-G13537.

The register describes a car that has been dressed up considerably: wide Giannini alloy wheels, a three-spoke steering wheel, red velvet seats, a speedometer to 150 mph, extra gauges under the dashboard and a 12V cigarette lighter.
