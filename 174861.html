What we know about this car

The register has 6 photos of this car, in a folder without an owner's name. The folder name gives registration NA-524342, colour white and first registration 14 June 1968. The registration is an Italian plate from the province of Naples (NA). Notable are VW-style indicators and an unusual ornament.

Chassis number 174861 has 6 digits, the usual numbers have 7. A digit is probably missing. Martin's VIN list had the same car a second time, with chassis ‘174861 (1 digit missing)’, the same registration date and the colour red. Both were merged into one line in October 2026. A note in the register says the car used to be red, which explains the colour difference.
