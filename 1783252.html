What we know about this car

The (masked) registration ROMA-xxxxx points to an Italian origin; the car is now in the United States. Worldwide Auctioneers sold it in September 2022 at the Auburn sale for US$20.160, with a mileage of about 17,369 miles.

The register links this car to the Tucson Auto Museum in Arizona. The same car may have taken part earlier in the Worldwide Auctioneers Texas Classic of April 2017 (US$26.400).
