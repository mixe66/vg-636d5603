What we know about this car

Historics sold this car in May 2022 for £17.546 and described it as ‘one lady owner from new’, always kept in south-east England. Untouched and original, with a light patina; the only thing ever done is respraying the wings red. Standard 499cc engine, number 110F0002113373.

Is the car still on the road?

No, not currently. DVLA check (29 August 2026): SORN, declared off the road. Last registration document 19 July 2022, shortly after the auction.

Distinguishing features

From the photo analysis: resprayed in a two-tone red-yellow scheme, different from the original single-colour factory paint. The register also mentions black bumpers and a silver grille insert by the headlamps.
