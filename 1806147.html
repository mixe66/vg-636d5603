What we know about this car

The register has 32 photos of this car. The Drive folder lists registration PE-158459, colour yellow, owner Simone and first registration 24 August 1968. Notable are a chrome grille insert and a ‘Gamine’ script in the grille.

The folder name began with 1800047. A screenshot from an Italian vehicle app (15 October 2026) shows chassis number (telaio) 1806147 and registration date 24/08/1968 for registration PE158459. Date and registration match the folder, the chassis number differs in two digits. Martin's VIN list already had an incomplete number 1806xxx for the same owner, colour and date, which fits 1806147.
