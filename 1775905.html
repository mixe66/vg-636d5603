What we know about this car

This car joined the Collection Catimini in 1993. The following year the bodywork was repaired and the whole car painted yellow, in a shade that was fitting at the time. In December 2018 the car had a brake overhaul, shortly before the sale. Engine number 110F0001881886, previous registration 4332-VT-49.

Notable: the French SIV check of August 2026 gives the colour as ‘blanc’, whereas the car has been yellow since 1994. The colour may never have been updated in the register.

Distinguishing features

From the photo analysis: a non-original, smooth silver grille insert instead of the standard vertically ribbed black insert. The register also mentions straps on the boot lid.
