What we know about this car

According to the auction description this car has stayed in the same family since 1979; the vendor bought it in 1998 and had it fully restored between 1998 and 2001, with the bodywork taken back to bare metal. The mileage of 18,700 is said to be original and was confirmed when the engine was dismantled. Colour scheme yellow with black interior, a factory combination.

The register notes an unrestored Ferrero steering wheel and an emergency battery switch in the boot, and a previous registration 4061-RN-83. Engine number 110F0001874245.
