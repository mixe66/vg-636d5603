What we know about this car

This car was sold new in Pescara in 1970 and used in Italy until 19 May 1971. It then went into a German collection, where it stayed from 1971 to 2012 (the previous registration HH-EM-875 confirms the Hamburg period). The current owner bought it in 2012. The car has not been restored since and is in original condition, with a 1971 bill of sale and German papers. Yellow with red interior, engine number 110F0001941218.

Is the car still on the road?

Yes, probably. DVLA check (29 August 2026): tax paid in the historic tax class, valid until 1 February 2027. No MOT requirement (40+). Insurance is not public in the UK.

Distinguishing features

From the photo analysis: an additional wing mirror fitted (two in total), a chrome roll bar behind the seats and a non-original ornament on the grille. The register mentions red wheels and extra lamps at the front.
