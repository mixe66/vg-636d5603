What we know about this car

This car was sold new in Sicily and was offered from Palermo on Bring a Trailer in 2019. The bodywork had been resprayed in the original colour orange; a custom two-tone black/orange trim had been fitted and it ran on 12-inch wheels from a 500R. Also a wooden steering wheel, a stamped Italian libretto and a copy of the original bill of sale. Mileage about 47,000 km.

The auction did not meet the reserve: the highest bid stalled at US$13.250. In the register the car is listed under the name of Filippo, with a map pin in Palermo. Engine number 110F0001954305.
