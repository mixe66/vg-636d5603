What we know about this car

The design of the Gamine inspired Noddy's red-and-yellow little car in the children's books. This particular car went a step further: it has been registered to and owned by Enid Blyton's company. The current owner bought it from the company that later took over the Blyton estate. The registration NOD-513 comes with it.

The car has a larger 594cc engine (number 4345512) with a synchromesh gearbox, a mileage of 4,553 miles and four owners in total. Marque specialist Middle Barton Garage maintained the car for a long time. Previous registration HSY774G.
