What we know about this car

This car was restored in 2005, in red with black vinyl. At the 2015 Artcurial auction it still had an Italian registration document; it is now registered in France (previous registration CZ-75707). The register notes a wooden steering wheel.

The French SIV check of August 2026 finds the registration DZ-950-BD active (Fiat 500 / Gamine, first registration 8 July 1968, colour rouge). So the car is in all likelihood still on the road.
