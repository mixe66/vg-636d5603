What we know about this car

This car was owned by Graham for years and had not been taxed since 1979: a genuine barn find. Trade Classics sold it online in March 2021 for £12.455. The car was then restored, including £7.000 of work at Tweenie Fiats in Swindon, and sold again at Historics in May 2024, for £11.949. After the restoration it therefore fetched slightly less than as an untouched find.

Red with yellow, black interior, engine number 110F0002102971. The name ‘Graham’ in the register is out of date; the car has changed owner twice since then.
