What we know about this car

This car was offered twice at Historics in 2023 and did not sell either time: on 22 July in Datchet (lot 155, estimate £20.000-25.000) and on 25 November in Weybridge (lot 209, estimate £17.000-23.000). Mileage about 54,000 miles, not guaranteed.

Shortly afterwards the car was exported. In the register the Drive folder was moved from the United Kingdom to Cyprus on 30 August 2026 and given an export marker. The car is listed under the name of Pash. Engine number 110F0002024409.

Is the car still on the road?

Unclear. DVLA check (29 August 2026): untaxed since 21 July 2026, export marker yes. The car is now in Cyprus, for which no public registration check exists.

Distinguishing features

The register mentions red piping on the seats, ‘vignale’ on the dashboard lid, seat belts and a ring around the boot lock.
