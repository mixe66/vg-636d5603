What we know about this car

Gooding & Company sold this car in January 2008 at the Scottsdale auction for US$28.600, within the estimate of US$25.000 to US$35.000. The lot description read ‘recently fully restored to showroom condition’. The auction house called the car a ‘1966’ at the time; the register keeps 1968, which fits this chassis number.

The chassis number appeared in the listing with the prefix 110M (110M1820976). Engine number 110F0001928647. The register records a Californian plate 6THE307.
