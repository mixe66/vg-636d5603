What we know about this car

The register has one photo of this car, in a folder without an owner's name. The chassis number in the folder name is incomplete (xx93078, the first digits are missing). The registration is ABY-571G and the colour orange. The folder has been under UK since 2020.

DVLA gives year of manufacture 1969 and first registration in July 1969, which fits the folder name (07-1969). DVLA lists the colour as ‘pink’, the register has ‘orange’. That may be a respray or a different name for the shade.

Auction history

A search for the registration ABY 571G (15 October 2026) found no auctions, listings or sales of this car. A Gamine with a different registration (AUC 749H) that sold at H&H in April 2026 is not this car.

Is the car still on the road?

Probably not. DVLA check (15 October 2026): untaxed since 1 April 2018, no MOT record (exempt, over 40 years old), export marker NO, latest registration certificate 20 October 2010. The car is therefore not recorded as exported, but has not been taxed for more than eight years.
