What we know about this car

In the register this car is listed under the name of Francois, with a French carte grise and a map pin near Cucq, close to Le Touquet. Auction house Osénat offered it in early 2024 as lot 211 and described it as ready for the Monte-Carlo Historique – a Gamine took part in the 1969 edition.

The registration 6936-VQ-75 is an old French FNI number. The French SIV check of August 2026 no longer finds it; with that old plate type that means little, because a car is allowed to keep running on its pre-2009 plate.

Distinguishing features
