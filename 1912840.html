What we know about this car

This car was reportedly imported to London about 25 years ago and at the time of sale had been with the same owner for 29 years. Mileage 56,919 miles. The car is on Borrani wheels and was supplied with a hood and three spare tyres. Only a UK registration document, no extensive history. Condition score 98 out of 135.

The original engine carried number 110F0002020899; it now has a 126 engine (126A5 000*4276850). The Drive folder name contains the names Gabby and Steve.
