What we know about this car

The Milanese auction house Art-Rite offered this car in an October auction (the exact year cannot be determined from the source, probably 2023 or 2024). Red with black interior, left-hand drive, with ‘original patina’ and sold new in Italy. The estimate was €25.000 to €35.000 and the lot was sold. The listing title says 1968; the register keeps 26 March 1970 as first registration.
