What we know about this car

The registration GGH-922J is a British number, but in the register the car is in France and is right-hand drive: a UK car that moved to France. Aguttes sold it in June 2024 at the Summer Sale in Paris for €10.004 including fees, the lowest result in this overview. Engine number 110F0002108878.

It is a considerably dressed-up car: twin exhaust, wide alloy wheels in black/silver, a chrome handbrake, non-original headlamp rings and missing Vignale badges.
